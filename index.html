Five Nights at Freddy's — Movie Poster

2025 / Typography, Photoshop

This is my project that I created in 2025 in my typography class using Photoshop. For this project, we had to select a movie we were interested in and make a movie poster out of it. The movie I chose to make was a Five Nights at Freddy's movie poster, which is also a video game.
